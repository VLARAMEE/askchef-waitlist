## 3. Information we collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically from your use of the Services, and from third parties you use in connection with AskChef.

### A. Information you provide directly

We may collect:

- **Account and profile information**, such as your name, email address, login credentials, sign-in provider information, account preferences, and profile settings.
- **Recipe and cooking content**, such as recipe URLs, pasted recipe text, imported recipe content, manually entered recipes, ingredient lists, steps, notes, cooking preferences, and saved recipe history.
- **Voice and text inputs**, such as questions you ask AskChef, text prompts, transcripts of voice interactions, and audio you choose to submit where voice features are available.
- **Images and uploads**, such as screenshots, cookbook photos, or other images and files you upload for recipe import or OCR-related features.
- **Support and communications**, such as messages you send to customer support, survey responses, waitlist submissions, or other communications with us.
- **Preferences**, such as unit preferences, temperature format, voice preferences, timer settings, and other product settings.
- **Information you choose to include in content**, which may include dietary preferences, allergy-related information, or other personal details if you include them in recipes, notes, or questions.

### B. Information we collect automatically

When you use the Services, we may automatically collect:

- **Device and technical information**, such as device type, operating system, browser type, app version, language, crash data, diagnostics, and similar technical identifiers.
- **Usage information**, such as pages viewed, screens visited, features used, session activity, timestamps, interactions, and referral information.
- **Log information**, such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP, access times, error logs, and security events.
- **Cookie and similar technology data** on the website, such as analytics data, session data, and preference data.

### C. Information from third parties

We may receive information from:

- authentication providers when you sign in using a third-party account;
- app stores and payment processors regarding subscription or purchase status;
- analytics, hosting, security, and infrastructure providers;
- speech, OCR, or AI service providers acting on our behalf; and
- your device or operating system when you use native features such as microphone, notifications, or Siri/Shortcuts integrations.
